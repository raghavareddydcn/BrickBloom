Open-structure coir chips for enhanced drainage, aeration, and soil blending in nursery media.

Why coir chips?

Applications

Use coir chips to improve media structure and drainage.

Orchid mixes

Airy, fast-draining substrate for epiphytes and premium ornamentals.

Soil blending

Add structure and stability to container substrates without losing moisture balance.

Propagation beds
